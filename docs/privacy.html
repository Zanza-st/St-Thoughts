PRIVACY POLICY

ST Thoughts · Last updated: May 31, 2026

ST Thoughts ("we", "our", "the app") is an idea-sharing network. This policy explains what data we collect, why, and how it is used.

1. Data We Collect

  • Account data: email address, handle, display name, role, and interests you provide during sign-up.
  • User-generated content: ideas, comments, offers, collab requests, and group activity you post.
  • Interaction data: votes, fires, ratings, and bookmarks you perform.
  • Device data: push notification token (if you grant permission), device type, and OS version.

2. How We Use Your Data

  • To operate the app and deliver its core features (feed, marketplace, groups, notifications).
  • To send push notifications about activity on your ideas (votes, comments, offers, collab requests).
  • To improve and maintain the service.

3. Data Storage

All data is stored on Supabase, a US-based cloud database provider. Supabase stores data on servers located in the United States. By using the app you consent to this transfer.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. Your handle, display name, and posted ideas are visible to other users of the app. Your email address is never shown publicly.

5. Push Notifications

If you grant notification permission, your Expo push token is stored on our servers and used solely to deliver in-app activity notifications. You can revoke permission at any time in your device settings.

6. Data Deletion

You may delete your account at any time from the Profile screen. Deleting your account removes your profile, ideas, comments, and all associated data from our servers within 30 days.

7. Children

ST Thoughts is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
