### Description (4000 chars max)
ST Thoughts is the idea network for people who think differently.

Drop a raw idea in seconds. The community votes, rates, and reacts — surfacing the concepts that actually deserve attention. Build a following around your thinking, not your face.

**POST IDEAS**
Write your idea. Choose a category — startup, creative, tech, commerce, film, or design. The feed is real-time; the best ideas rise to the top.

**MARKET YOUR IDEAS**
List any idea for sale or open it to barter. Set an asking price, describe what you'd trade for, or both. Receive and review offers directly in the app.

**FIND YOUR COLLABORATORS**
Mark an idea as open to collab and specify what roles you need. Builders, designers, investors, and operators can request to join. Say yes to the right people.

**PRIVATE GROUPS**
Create invite-only groups for your team, mastermind, or inner circle. Ideas posted to a group stay private to its members.

**STAY IN THE LOOP**
Real-time notifications for every vote, comment, offer, collab request, and trade. Nothing slips through.

Whether you're an idea person, a builder, an investor, or a collaborator — ST Thoughts is where raw ideas find the people they need.
